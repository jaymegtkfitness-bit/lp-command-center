The Standards of Excellence
1

Own it.

Your results are yours. We give you everything you need. Nobody else can eat, train, or sleep for you.

2

Information was never your problem. You do not have to learn it all before you start. Do the next step, then the next.

3

Ask early, ask once, then move.

Stuck for more than a day? Ask. Do not sit in silence and do not wait for perfect. Get the answer and take the action.

4

Share your wins.

Post them. Your win is someone else's proof that this works, and it carries the room on the days they are flat.

5

Protect the room.

What is shared here stays here. Real talk only happens where people feel safe. Guard that for everyone.

6

Show up.

Book your calls and keep them. Momentum is the whole game. Speed matters more than perfection.

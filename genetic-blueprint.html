Before you decide

Questions people ask first.

How does the test work?

You get a simple at-home kit in the mail. Swab the inside of your cheek, seal it, and send it back in the prepaid mailer. An accredited lab analyzes your sample, and your personalized Blueprint comes back to you. No blood, no clinic visit.

Is this a medical or disease-risk test?

No. The Genetic Blueprint is a wellness and performance tool. It looks at markers related to nutrition, fitness, and recovery so we can personalize your plan. It is not a diagnostic test, it does not assess disease risk, and it is not medical advice. If you have a medical condition, keep working with your physician.

How long until I get my results?

Turnaround depends on the lab, typically a few weeks from when your sample arrives. As soon as your Blueprint is back, we schedule your 1:1 walkthrough.

Do I have to be a coaching client?

No. Anyone can get the Blueprint. It makes your Legacy Performance dashboard and plan far more personalized, but it stands on its own too.

Is my genetic data private?

Yes. Your sample and results are handled confidentially through the lab's secure process. Your Blueprint is yours.

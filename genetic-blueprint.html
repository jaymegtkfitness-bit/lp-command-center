Before you decide

Questions people ask first.

How does the test work?

You get a simple at-home kit in the mail. Provide a quick saliva sample and a simple finger-prick, seal them, and send them back in the prepaid mailer. An accredited lab reads your DNA and your biomarkers, and your personalized Blueprint comes back to you. No clinic visit.

Is this a medical or disease-risk test?

No. The Genetic Blueprint is a wellness and performance tool. It reads your DNA and biomarkers to personalize your nutrition, supplements, training, and recovery. It is not a diagnostic test, it does not assess disease risk, and it is not medical advice. Any peptide options are provider-guided and informational, and eligibility and dosing are determined solely by the licensed medical team. If you have a medical condition, keep working with your physician.

How long until I get my results?

Turnaround is usually about four weeks from when your samples arrive. You don't wait on it though. Your Foundations Protocol and Complete Nutrition System start the week you buy, and as soon as your Blueprint is back we go over it together.

Do I have to be a coaching client?

No. Anyone can get their Genetic Blueprint. It feeds your Legacy Performance dashboard and makes your plan more personalized. Every dollar you invest also goes toward your Built 4 More coaching if you continue, but it is not mandatory.

Is my genetic data private?

Yes. Your sample and results are handled confidentially through the lab's secure process. Your Blueprint is yours.

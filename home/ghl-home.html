FAQs

What is Legacy Performance?

A coaching company built around one idea: you don't have a knowledge problem, you have an awareness problem.

You already run systems for your calendar, your budget and your household. We build you one for your body, one that survives a real week instead of a motivated one.

Are the numbers really free?

Yes. Answer a few honest questions and your calorie target, protein floor, macros and realistic pace show up on screen. No card, no payment.

If you want the Complete Nutrition System after that, it's optional.

Is the Complete Nutrition System a one-time meal plan?

No. One meal plan gets you to one goal. This is your own system that builds your meal plans for you, and it's yours for life.

Tired of your meals? Schedule changed? New phase? Build a new plan any time. Your numbers move with you, and your restaurant orders and swaps always match them.

What's included?

Protein on autopilot. Nourishment made simple. Carbs and fats, done for you. Meal plans for every goal. A shopping list, shopping guide and meal prep guide. Swaps for anything. No more guessing at restaurants. A weekly check-in that gets smarter with the LP Coach. And the full system done for you, then perfected together over 30 days.

It's built within 2 business days, it's yours for life, and it's backed by a 30-day guarantee.

Is this a diet?

No. A diet is a to-do list with an end date. This is a functional health system that gets you to your goal, and one you never have to quit, fail or start over. When your life changes, it changes with you.

Most people are surprised how much food is in it. Almost everyone we coach came in eating too little.

What is the free community?

The Legacy Performance Project on Skool. A weekly Wednesday coaching call, self-paced courses, and people running the same system you are.

Who is Built 4 More for?

People ready for private, one-on-one coaching. It's the best fit if you've dieted before, you can track your food, and you're already training.

It starts with a conversation, so book a call and we'll see if it's the right next step.

About the founder

I spent about ten years coaching and competing in CrossFit and competed at 185 pounds. I could out-train almost anyone in the room, and I still ended up at 242.

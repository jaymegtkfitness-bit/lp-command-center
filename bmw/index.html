BMW Assessment

Your BMWAssessment.

Body. Move. Work. This is your starting line. It shows me how your body moves, where it's strong, and where we build first. We run it again every quarter so you can see the change.

What you need: about 45 minutes, your phone to film, a little floor space, a bench or sturdy bed, a pull-up bar if you have one, and a friend if you can grab one (filming is easier with help). Do it in comfortable clothes you can move in.
The one rule: if anything hurts at any point, stop that test and score it 0. Don't push through pain. Any 0 means check in with a doctor or physical therapist before we load that movement.

M · Move

Five movement screens

Film each one, then score yourself. Be honest. A 2 isn't bad, it's information.

Scoring for every screen: 3 = clean · 2 = you can do it, but something compensates · 1 = you can't complete it · 0 = pain at any point.

1. Split squat

Set up: sit on the floor with your legs straight. Put a marker at your hip and one at your feet. Stand up, put your front toes at one marker and your back toes at the other, then do a split squat.

Film: from the side, each side.

What's scored: back knee near the floor · front heel stays down · knee tracks over your toes · torso upright · no wobble.

2. Overhead squat

Set up: feet about shoulder width, arms straight overhead. Squat as deep as you can and stand back up.

Film: 3 reps from the side, then 3 reps facing the camera.

What's scored: thighs below parallel · heels stay down · knees over your feet · arms stay overhead.

3. Scratch test

Set up: make two fists. Reach one fist over your shoulder and down your back, and the other up your back from below. Try to bring them together. Then switch sides.

Film: from behind, 3 reps each side (right hand on top, then left hand on top).

What's scored: fists within about one hand-length = 3 · within one and a half = 2 · farther apart = 1.

4. Standing toe touch

Set up: feet together, knees straight. Reach down toward your toes, hold 3 seconds at the bottom, stand back up.

Film: from the side, 2 to 3 reps.

What's scored: fingertips pass your toes · knees stay straight · hips shift back · smooth spine.

5. Lying straight-leg raise

Set up: lie on your back, legs straight. Keeping one leg flat on the floor, lift the other leg as high as you can with the knee straight. Hold 2 seconds, lower it.

Film: from the side, 3 lifts each leg.

What's scored: raised ankle passes the middle of the thigh on the down leg = 3 · reaches the knee line = 2 · below the knee = 1. The down leg has to stay flat.

Sorensen test (back-extension hold)

Lie face down on a bench or bed with your hips at the edge and your upper body hanging off. Have someone hold your legs down. Arms crossed on your chest, lift your upper body until you're level (a straight line) and hold. Stop when you can't stay level. Skip it if your back hurts.

Questions

Before you start

What happens first?

You fill out the intake, which takes about five minutes. Your DNA kit ships in week one, and week one your only job is to eat the way you actually eat and log three honest days. That recall is what your system gets built from, so the week is the work, not a wait.

Your calls start the week of November 2nd, and that is when I hand you the built system.

How long until my genetic results come back?

Your kit arrives in 5 to 7 business days, and results come back 2 to 4 weeks after the lab has your sample. You are eating to a real plan the whole time.

Is the genetic test bloodwork?

No. It is a saliva kit and it reads your genetics. Your genetics do not change, which is why it is a one-time read you keep.

Is this a medical test?

No. It is an educational tool used to personalize nutrition, training and supplementation. It is not a diagnosis and it does not replace your doctor.

What happens after the four weeks?

You keep all of it. The app is lifetime, and the system, the generator and your results are yours.

The last call is where we decide your next move together: the phase you are running, what it is going to ask of you, and how you want to go about it. Some people take it from there and run it themselves, which is the whole point of building you a system. Some would rather have someone still reading their week. Both are real answers, and we pick yours on that call.

Can I split the payment?

Yes. Two payments of $597, one today and one 30 days later. Everything starts the same day either way: your kit ships in week one and your calls are booked the same as anyone paying in full.

Why track for a week if you already built my plan?

Because a food recall is what you remember, and a fully tracked week is what actually happened. Seven days of food, water, steps, sleep, training and weight tells us whether your metabolism has genuinely adapted or whether the starting number just needed moving. Those two problems have opposite solutions, and guessing between them is how people end up eating less and less for years.

What if I have never tracked my food?

That is the gap this closes. Your numbers are already set and your meals are already chosen, so you are following a plan rather than logging into a void.

Three things decide your timeline

Every fat-loss plan is built from three variables. The work: how many pounds you want to lose. The timeline: how long that honestly takes. The intensity: how deep the deficit runs. The work is fixed. So timeline and intensity trade against each other. Cut harder and it goes faster, until it does not, because you get hungry, tired, stall out and quit.

Our rule is simple. The intensity has a ceiling. When the math does not fit the date you wanted, the timeline moves, never the deficit.

How we estimate your pace

Your maintenance is built from your goal weight times 15. Lean starts at goal weight times 13, and steps to times 12 only when the scale stops setting new lows. The gap between the two, over a week, divided by the 3,500 calories in a pound of fat, is your pace. We keep it between 0.3 and 1.5 pounds a week. Slower than that is a stall. Faster costs muscle.

Steps help. Every day above about 5,000 steps adds a little burn, and you can see it move your weeks with the slider. Steps support the deficit. Nutrition creates it.

Why seasons, not one long diet

No single diet should run past about 16 weeks. After that, hunger climbs, energy drops, training suffers and the odds of rebounding go way up. So a bigger goal is split into Lean seasons with Sustain stretches in between, where you eat at maintenance for your goal body and let things settle. It feels slower. It is the version that actually finishes.

Read it as a range

The shorter number is the pace you might hold on a great run. The longer one is the pace most people actually live. Both are estimates. Your weekly weigh-ins, read as a two-week trend, are what tell you where you really are. One day is noise. Two weeks is a trend.

Questions people ask

How long will it take to reach my goal weight?

Divide the pounds you want to lose by a sustainable weekly pace. We estimate yours from your goal weight: most people land between about 0.5 and 1 pound a week. Losing 35 pounds toward a 145 pound goal takes roughly 41 to 61 weeks of Lean, plus the Sustain breaks between seasons.

How fast is it safe to lose weight?

We cap estimates at 1.5 pounds a week, and less for smaller goals. Faster than that and a larger share of what you lose is muscle, which is exactly what you want to keep.

What is a Lean season?

A fat-loss phase of up to 16 weeks. Bigger goals use more than one, with Sustain stretches at maintenance in between so your body and your life get a break.

Do steps really change my timeline?

Yes, a little. Steps above about 5,000 a day add burn and can shave weeks off. But nutrition creates the deficit. Steps support it.

Why is my date later than other calculators?

Many calculators assume you hold an aggressive deficit forever. We plan the pace most people can keep, and we build in seasons, because a date you actually reach beats a faster one you abandon.

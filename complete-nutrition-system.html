The Power Food System gives you your targets and the framework to run yourself. The Complete Nutrition System is the done-for-you version. I build the entire plan and a real 14-day meal plan around the foods you already eat, so you follow it instead of figuring it out.

For most women 35 to 55, yes. Years of under-eating and heavy cardio adapt your metabolism down. We feed your goal weight, anchor protein, and let your body stop fighting you. Missy, Kristen, and Britney all ate more and got leaner. It's not a trick. It's the sequencing.

No. Your dessert budget and a flexible day structure are built into the plan on purpose. You keep the foods that make your life yours. The system is built to hold with them in it, not to pretend they don't exist.

How much time does this actually take?

The build is done for you, so the work on your end is following it and logging your food, which takes a few minutes a day in your app. No cooking two separate meals. No spreadsheets.

What if my foods or my schedule change?

The plan is built to flex, and you get the weekly adjustment method so you always know the next move. And if the build itself doesn't fit your real life, I rebuild it until it does.

The system is built around nourishing your body and your goal weight, which is exactly the approach that helped clients restore a cycle and rebuild energy. It is a nutrition system, not medical treatment. If you have a diagnosed condition, keep working with your physician alongside it.

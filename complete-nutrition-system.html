Questions women ask me first.

How is this different from the free Power Food System?

The Power Food System gives you your targets and the framework to run yourself. The Complete Nutrition System is the done-for-you version, and it goes deeper. It starts with a more detailed intake that captures things the free tool never asks for, so the plan is built around your real life, not just your numbers. From there I build your full phase structure, mapping how your calories and macros shift as your body responds over the weeks, plus a real 14-day meal plan in the foods you already eat. You follow it instead of figuring it out.

I've always eaten less to lose weight. You want me to eat more?

For most women 35 to 55, yes. Years of under-eating and heavy cardio adapt your metabolism down. And here's what most people miss: when you undereat, you're usually not nourishing yourself either, just eating less of everything. So we aim to eat more while keeping your calories right for your goal. That gives your body the energy it needs and the real nourishment it's been missing. We feed your goal weight, anchor protein, and let your body stop fighting you. Missy, Kristen, and Britney all ate more and got leaner. It's not a trick. It's the sequencing.

Do I have to give up wine or dessert?

No. Your dessert budget and a flexible day structure are built into the plan on purpose. You keep the foods that make your life yours. The system is built to hold with them in it, not to pretend they don't exist.

How much time does this actually take?

The build is done for you, so the work on your end is following it and logging your food, which takes a few minutes a day in your app. No cooking two separate meals. No spreadsheets.

What if my foods or my schedule change?

The plan is built to flex, and you get the weekly adjustment method so you always know the next move. And if the build itself doesn't fit your real life, I rebuild it until it does.

Will this work for my hormones, my age, or my thyroid?

The system is built around nourishing your body and your goal weight, which is exactly the approach that helped clients restore a cycle and rebuild energy. It is a nutrition system, not medical treatment. If you have a diagnosed condition, keep working with your physician alongside it.

You can keep rebuilding a plan every few weeks. Or you can have it built once, around your real life, and spend your energy actually living it.
